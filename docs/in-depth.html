The Core

Four design principles

CAPAS makes scientific claim evaluation auditable, repeatable and ready to integrate.

Deterministic verdicts

The same claim and evidence bundle always produces the same verdict, with no probabilistic outputs and no model drift. Schema v3 rules are the single source of truth.

No randomness

Schema-validated structure

Every payload is validated against CAPAS Schema v3 before the verdict. Field types, required keys and claim-type constraints are enforced at the gate boundary.

CAPAS Schema v3

Full provenance trail

Every REJECT and HOLD verdict includes machine-readable provenance blockers, exportable as structured JSON: licensing flags, reproducibility gaps, attestation failures.

Audit-ready

Zero LLM dependency

CAPAS does not use language models for gate decisions. Deterministic rule functions, 26 cross-domain invariant gates plus per-claim-type evidence contracts, compute every verdict offline. Every decision carries a re-derivable audit_hash.

Rule-based only
Position in the stack

Generation systems answer “what can I say”. CAPAS answers “should this have been said”.

Frontier models and agents generate claims. Adjacent systems each cover one slice: LLM judges score truth stochastically and without replay, fact-checkers assess truth rather than the claim boundary, domain validators such as Pinnacle 21 cover one field. None decides, deterministically and replayably, whether declared evidence licenses a claim before reuse. The engine registers 14 claim types; any other type returns HOLD.

Cross-domain engine

One mechanism. 10 domains. 26 gates.

A fabricated claim still has to satisfy the conservation laws of its field, and those laws are re-derivable without an oracle. The same engine catches a balance sheet that does not close and an impossible survey mean (GRIM). It catches a 99%-sensitive test read as 99% PPV for a rare disease, an unbalanced reaction, a non-dimensional equation and a qubit with T2 > 2·T1. A number that breaks a domain law forces REJECT; the check is downgrade-only. The fail-closed invariant is test-locked: 18/18 structurally deficient claims kept out of ACCEPT.

Re-derivation

The engine re-computes the claimed result from its raw inputs.

Above the schema gate, CAPAS re-computes the claimed result from its raw inputs. What it reproduces it marks GATE. What it cannot re-derive it marks ATTEST: signed and bound, never presented as verified. The boundary is explicit on every receipt.

GATE = re-derived and reproducible. ATTEST = signed, not verified. CAPAS certifies computational consistency of the re-derivable slice, not scientific truth or the authenticity of raw data (the GIGO residual).

WHAT IS NOT VALIDATED

The GIGO ceiling. CAPAS decides on the structure of evidence, not ground truth: a self-consistent, well-formed, fabricated payload can pass the gate. The fuzz and pedagogy-governance tests report this residual as a disclosed false-admit. The gate raises the cost of lying and makes every verdict re-derivable; it does not detect a careful liar.

  • CAPAS is not a fraud detector; the measurement follows. On 888 clean control papers versus 6,348 papers retracted for data, recomputable numeric inconsistencies did not separate the groups: 2.13% severe in clean, 1.58% in retracted. No oracle-adjudicated fraud-detection rate exists; the measured figure is the blind self-gating corpus above. python3 research/retraction_audit/grade_severity.py
  • Cross-model adversarial review (GOVERNANCE §6, eight runs, 2026-10-13): a model of another family attacked 7 of 10 claims and refuted 3: a scope false-accept, an unsealed figure, an unversioned grading rule. Each was corrected and frozen as a test or a sealed file the same day. Transcripts in research/section6_package/. Nothing is marked proved.
  • The cross-domain blind-adjudication study (Gap-1) has not been run. Its registry, receipts and gating are built and pass (python3 benchmarks/study_assembly.py); the n≥500 human-adjudicated comparison is pre-registered, not yet executed.
  • No SOC 2 / ISO 27001 certification. Roadmap, not held.
  • The head-to-head is retired as evidence. Against the real LLM judge the two tied on a 10-claim corpus; the UMA, BTS and EigenTrust baselines were Krenn·IQ models.

Developer integration

A verification layer around your LLM.

An installable verification layer: your model proposes, CAPAS disposes. It never returns ACCEPT for a claim whose required evidence fields are missing or contradicted (18/18 held). On blind claims, 4 of 21 bad out-of-sample claims were accepted.

$ pip install capas-claim-gate
# live on PyPI · or from source: pip install git+https://github.com/fomv9354lve/capas-inteligentes
from capas_sdk import verified
# your model proposes the evidence; CAPAS gates it
verdict = verified(my_llm, "reproducibility_check")["verdict"]
# -> ACCEPT / REWRITE / REJECT / HOLD (never the LLM’s call)

The gain is reliability, not capability: the output becomes admissible or deferred. Scope: CAPAS grounds record↔text, not text↔reality; a source that misreports its methods and withholds its data passes (the GIGO ceiling).

Submission-stage paper

Audit of a submission-stage paper: the gate flagged one verified slip in a 13-row table.

CAPAS parses a paper’s tables from the PDF with deterministic text extraction; no language model re-types the numbers. It re-derives every closed-form claim from the paper’s stated formula and flags any tabulated value that conflicts with it. On an inverse-Ginibre random-matrix paper with a 13-row coefficient table, it re-derived all 13 rows. It flagged one whose tabulated prediction diverged from the closed form by 2.6%: a referee-relevant slip, invisible inside the self-consistent row.

Standard and certificate

The intended asset is a conformance mark and a signed certificate that a buyer re-runs rather than trusts. It requires an executed charter and third-party adjudication; neither has happened yet.

CAPAS is built so that trust rests on re-runs rather than assertions. Every verdict re-derives to a hash an independent party can reproduce. Every headline claim is CLOSED / BACKED / SCOPED in a public ledger that discloses which numbers are synthetic. The mark attests only that an artifact passed a suite you run yourself with the same verdict and hash, issued as a signed, content-addressed certificate. A competitor can copy a gate in a week; it cannot copy a self-run conformance mark, a signed-certificate audit trail and a relicensing posture renounced in writing. Whether this becomes the reference standard depends on an independent external audit, which has not been done.

Open standard · governed

Open engine, with a charter that pre-commits the mark to neutral governance.

CAPAS ships open-core under Apache-2.0: schema, calculus, reference gate, CLI, tests and benchmark corpus are free to run and fork. The defensible asset is the certification mark, and a mark earns trust only if it cannot be withdrawn. CAPAS is used as a project name; no trademark is registered. The charter pre-commits the mark to neutral governance before adoption. That commitment let Open Policy Agent survive its sponsor’s acquisition. Elastic, HashiCorp and Redis were forked after relicensing; MongoDB lost its OSI standing. Krenn·IQ renounces that option in writing. Governance charter →

First market

Statistical-claim admissibility for regulated submissions.

Pinnacle 21 checks whether a trial dataset is structurally well-formed (CDISC conformance), not whether the reported statistic is licensed by its evidence. CAPAS checks that: significance versus alpha, multiplicity, confidence interval excluding the null, effect direction, endpoint pre-specification, re-derivably and beside the submission. The pharma gate is a library function (capas_pharma.gate_pharma_stat_claim), not on the hosted API. On a 3,024-case synthetic admissibility corpus it accepted 0 deficient claims; this is contract coverage of the space P21 skips, not a false-accept rate on production submissions.
