Brief

Deterministic claim admissibility
for regulated statistical-evidence review.

CAPAS is a deterministic gate before scientific claims enter reports, governed datasets, or fine-tuning preparation. It checks whether supplied evidence licenses a claim for controlled reuse and returns a replayable packet: verdict, reason, required evidence contract, no-LLM marker, and fine-tune readiness.

Decision path

An LLM may be used upstream to extract the payload from a paper or to draft a rewrite suggestion. It is never used to determine admissibility. Versioned deterministic rules over the supplied evidence fields produce the verdict (ACCEPT / REWRITE / REJECT / HOLD). The non_claim field is the machine-readable marker. The REWRITE wording CAPAS returns (licensed_claim) is a deterministic template from the evidence fields; text a model redrafts upstream or downstream is not CAPAS output.

Text-ingested claims also carry source evidence spans. /api/decide and /api/gate return unsigned packets whose audit_hash anyone re-derives. A signed, content-addressed certificate (capas_certstore) is issued only through the auth-gated issuance path on the Security page.

Fine-tune readiness

The 14 fine-tune readiness criteria.

An ACCEPTed claim may still not qualify as training data. These 14 checks, copied from the engine, decide fine_tune_ready after an ACCEPT without changing the verdict. Four criteria are self-declared flags the engine checks only for true. Five are engine-verified; four of those five require a deployment the hosted API lacks: CAPAS_VERIFY_REMOTE_SOURCES=1 for URL recovery, plus local registries (witness registry, reviewer attestations, RO-Crate files) absent from the pip wheel. On the hosted API a payload reaches at most 10/14, so fine_tune_ready is a library-mode property.

verdict_accept · the claim verdict is ACCEPT
schema_clean · no schema or required-field blockers
source_backed_evidence self-declared · source-backed evidence is attached
external_review self-declared · external review is attached
semantic_alignment self-declared · claim text alignment is self-declared, not externally certified
witness_independence self-declared · witness independence is self-declared, not externally certified
provenance_sources · provenance sources / source URLs present
review_hash_verified engine-verified · review hash matches the review packet
source_urls_recoverable_hashable engine-verified · source URLs recoverable with matching hashes
witness_registry_resolved engine-verified · witness ID resolves in the registry
ro_crate_validated engine-verified · RO-Crate packet valid and hash-matched
reviewer_attestation_verified engine-verified · reviewer identity / attestation verifiable
review_id_present · provenance review_id is present

Mechanism

Evidence contracts decide licensed scope.

The gate checks a claim and its evidence package against a claim-type evidence contract. The contract defines the required evidence and the licensed scope. The gate returns a deterministic verdict.

Regulatory context

An auditable evidence trail for training-data governance.

AI-governance frameworks ask teams to document the quality and provenance of the data behind a claim. CAPAS produces that artifact per claim, deterministically, with no model in the decision path. It supports compliance and review work; it does not certify compliance.

EU AI Act · Art. 10

Data governance

Article 10 requires high-risk AI systems to use training, validation, and testing data that meet quality and governance criteria. CAPAS records, per claim, whether supplied evidence licenses reuse: a traceable check when data enters a dataset.

NIST AI RMF

Traceability

The NIST AI Risk Management Framework emphasizes documentation and traceability across the data lifecycle. Every CAPAS decision emits a reason, evidence spans, provenance blockers, and an audit hash, reviewable after the fact.

No LLM judge

Deterministic by design

Unlike LLM-as-judge evaluators, CAPAS has no language model in the decision path, so the same payload yields the same verdict and any decision can be re-run and audited independently.

Regulatory references are context only. CAPAS does not certify compliance with the EU AI Act, the NIST AI RMF, or any other framework; it produces auditable decision artifacts that support governance and review.

Two-week pilot

Claim drift measured on your own corpus.

A controlled operating test: which claims are licensed, which must be rewritten, which must be rejected, and which need more evidence before reuse.

Steps
1

Corpus: pharma statistical-evidence review, the first market. Other verticals follow only after the pilot passes its gate.

2

The Pilot page protocol: records from the sponsor’s own history, where a bad record is one its reviewers later corrected or rejected; nothing seeded; two blind coders; engine frozen at a tag; verdicts sealed by hash before adjudication.

3

Pre-registered go / no-go: false-accept ≤ 30% and false-reject ≤ 25% on at least 40 bad and 40 good adjudicated records (Wilson 95%), κ ≥ 0.70 between coders. Otherwise no-go. Full gate and power →

4

Report in the sealed-batch template: decision mix, coder agreement, false-accept and false-reject with intervals, HOLD share, exclusions and provenance blockers.

ACCEPT

Licensed for controlled reuse. Fine-tune obligations to clear.

REWRITE

Evidence supports a narrower claim. Returns the licensed boundary.

REJECT

Returns which evidence is missing or failing.

HOLD

Returns the steps: supply the missing field, verify provenance, resubmit.

Disclaimers

Required context for buyers.

·

CAPAS evaluates supplied evidence fields only; it does not infer hidden evidence, provide legal advice, certify broad scientific truth, or replace external review.

·

The 1,238 decisions and the 78% REWRITE-or-REJECT share are reproducible from the engine benchmark (benchmarks/family_decision_mix.py) over a synthetic decision-space grid. They are not a production drift rate; production rates require an independently adjudicated corpus.

·

Do not share payload URLs or exports containing confidential source text, reviewer IDs, witness IDs, licensed materials, or proprietary provenance paths without authorization. Data handling & security →

  • CAPAS evaluates supplied evidence fields only; it does not infer hidden evidence, provide legal advice, certify broad scientific truth, or replace external review.
  • The decision packet and the four reference decisions on this page are engine output, regenerated and checked in CI (verify_brief_packet_matches_engine.py, home_gate_decisions.py). Schema, evidence contracts, and blockers are emitted by the deterministic gate; no model judgment is involved.
  • Pilot and capacity figures are planning models and must be calibrated against the organization's own review baseline. A saving is the difference between two complete processes, each including evidence preparation, normalization, exception review and adjudication. Subtracting gate time from human time does not measure it.
