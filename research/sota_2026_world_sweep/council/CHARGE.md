# Council charge — integrate the SOTA, strengthen CAPAS, converge on how to proceed

Owner's words (Osvaldo, 2026-10-14): "reconozcamos lo que hay, integramos lo que falta, fortalecemos para ser referente …
si me paré tan lejos es porque me paré sobre hombros de gigantes … enfoquémonos ahora en la herramienta … itera con un
equipo que tenga estos referentes hasta que todos estén de acuerdo en cómo seguir; tu plan de 5 pasos es buena base."

## Inputs every council member reads first
1. `../SYNTHESIS.md` — the comparative synthesis (§1–§9; §9 is the 5-action plan under review).
2. `../BRIEF.md` — what CAPAS is and its measured limits (never argue against the measured limits; argue about the plan).
3. The wave file for the country/tradition you represent (`../wave_*.md`), for exact citations.
4. The live state of the tool: `../../../CLAUDE.md` §3b, `../../../docs/CODING_GUIDE_EVIDENCE_FIELDS.md` (ingestion
   adapters), `../../../capas_ingest.py`, `../../../docs/proof_ledger.json` (23 entries), `../../../research/atlas_corpus/README.md`.

## What each member must deliver (round 1): file `round1_<persona>.md`, ≤ 900 words, this structure
1. **Giants I bring** — 3–6 works/tools/standards from my tradition that CAPAS must credit by name (with URL) and WHAT
   exactly CAPAS inherits from each (a rule, a format, a protocol, a number).
2. **Critique of the 5-action plan** — action by action: keep / change / drop, with the reason, and the concrete test
   that would prove it done. Add at most 2 actions my tradition considers missing.
3. **Integration order** — my proposed sequence for the next 90 days, focused on the tool (code, tests, ledger entries),
   not on marketing.
4. **Where CAPAS is ahead of my tradition** (one paragraph, honest) and **where my tradition is ahead** (one paragraph).
5. **Red lines** — what I would refuse to sign (max 3).
6. **Positioning as a reference** — how do the leaders of my tradition position themselves (venues, networks, standards
   bodies, registries), and the one concrete step CAPAS should take there.

Rules: cite only what is in the wave files or in the repo (no new web research in round 1); never fabricate a URL;
disagree sharply where you disagree — the owner wants convergence through argument, not politeness.
